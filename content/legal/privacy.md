This policy explains how Permit Inc. and its affiliate Permit.io Ltd. ("Permit", "we", "us") collect and use information through Phean. Phean is a shared task and goal ledger for people and the AI agents they connect to it. The policy covers the website at phean.ai and the Phean service (the "Service") that we provide to the people and organizations who create workspaces in it ("Customers"), their invited members, and the agents they connect ("Users").

By creating an account or using the Service, you acknowledge that we process your information as this policy describes.

## Information we process

**Account information.** You create an account with your name, email address and a password, which we store only as a salted hash. If you sign in with GitHub or Google instead, we receive your name, email address, profile picture and whether the provider has verified your email. We never receive your password for those services. We call this "Account Information".

**Workspace content.** Everything people and agents put into a workspace: tasks, goals, notes and memory, documents and files, the questions agents ask and the answers people give, links to agent sessions, and the activity log of who changed what. We process workspace content on behalf of the Customer who owns the workspace, to provide the Service to them. We call this "Workspace Content".

**Member and invitation information.** The names, email addresses and roles of the people in a workspace, and the email addresses of people invited to join one.

**Agent and integration information.** The name and level of each agent connected to a workspace, the person who approved it, its OAuth client details, and hashed access keys. For webhooks, we keep the URL you register and a log of recent deliveries.

**Session and security information.** When you sign in, we record your IP address, your browser's user agent and the times you signed in, to keep sessions secure, to limit repeated sign-in attempts, and to investigate abuse.

**Usage information.** If you allow analytics, we use Google Analytics 4 to learn how the website and the Service are used. It receives the pages you visit: on the public website their address, and in the Service only the kind of page (such as "a task page"), without workspace names or ids; which features you use (such as answering a decision, or switching the board to the calendar); your device type, browser, screen size and approximate location, as Google Analytics collects them; and, if you are signed in, a hashed random id that stands for you without naming you. It never receives your name, your email address, the titles of tasks, goals or documents, or anything that people or agents write in Phean. We call this "Usage Information".

**Inquiries.** If you write to us, we keep your name, email address, the content of your message and anything else you choose to send.

Giving us this information is not a legal requirement, but without Account Information we cannot create your account or provide the Service.

## How we use information

We use information to:

- create and secure your account, and sign you in;
- provide the Service to Customers, including showing workspace content to the people and agents a Customer has let in, and delivering the webhooks a Customer has set up;
- respond to your questions and requests;
- understand, if you allow analytics, which pages and features are used, so we can improve them;
- maintain and improve the Service, assure its quality, troubleshoot problems, prevent fraud and abuse, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms;
- comply with the law.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use Workspace Content to train AI models. We send you marketing only with your prior consent.

## Who we share information with

We do not share personal information with third parties except:

- **Service providers** that process it for us under contract, only to run the Service. Today these are Vercel, which hosts the Service and stores uploaded files, Neon, which hosts our database, and, if you allow analytics, Google, which processes Usage Information for us through Google Analytics (see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)).
- **Sign-in providers.** If you choose to sign in with GitHub or Google, that provider learns that you signed in to Phean.
- **Agents and integrations that a workspace connects.** An agent approved for a workspace, and a webhook its Customer registers, receive the workspace content that the Customer's settings allow. The Customer decides which agents and webhooks to connect.
- **Our corporate group**, meaning Permit Inc. and Permit.io Ltd., for internal operations only.
- **Authorities and advisers**, where the law requires it, or where someone abuses the Service or breaks the law and we need legal counsel or the help of the authorities.
- **A successor**, if we are part of a merger, acquisition or other corporate change, as that change requires.

## Cookies

Phean uses cookies that it needs to work:

- a session cookie that keeps you signed in;
- a preference cookie (`phean-appearance`) that remembers whether you chose light, dark or system appearance;
- preference cookies that remember how you laid out views such as the board;
- a preference cookie (`phean-consent`) that remembers whether you allowed analytics, for a year.

With your consent, Phean also uses Google Analytics cookies: `_ga`, which holds a random id for your browser, and `_ga_<id>`, which keeps the state of your visit. Google Analytics loads only after you accept it in the bar that asks when you first visit; until then no Google script runs and nothing is sent to Google. We use it for analytics alone: no advertising, no ad personalization, and Google signals are off. You can change your mind at any time with "Analytics choice" in the site's footer or the Analytics card on your account page. Declining, there or in the bar, stops analytics and deletes the Google Analytics cookies. After you sign up, sign in or create a workspace, a short-lived cookie (`phean-ga-signal`, ten minutes) tells the next page which of those happened, so it can be counted; it holds only the kind of event, and is set only if you allowed analytics.

We do not use advertising cookies. You can delete or block cookies in your browser's settings, but you will then have to sign in again, and Phean will not be able to keep you signed in.

## Security and retention

We protect information with encryption in transit, hashed passwords and keys, access checks on every workspace, and limits on repeated sign-in attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise that information will never be accessed without permission.

We keep Account Information while your account exists. We keep Workspace Content until the Customer, or a person or agent with permission, deletes it, or until the workspace is deleted. Webhook delivery logs are kept for 7 days. Google Analytics keeps Usage Information for 14 months. We may keep information longer where we must, to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

## International transfers

We store and process information in the United States, and our service providers may process it in other countries. Where the law requires it, we protect those transfers with appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Your rights

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or a place with similar laws, Permit is the controller of your Account Information. For Workspace Content, the Customer who owns the workspace is the controller and we process it on their behalf, so please send requests about it to that Customer first.

We rely on these legal bases: performing our contract with you (Account Information and providing the Service), our legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, improving the Service, answering inquiries, and corporate changes), complying with the law, and your consent (marketing and analytics).

You may have the right to access your information, correct it, receive a copy of it, object to or restrict its processing, have it erased, and withdraw consent you have given. To use any of these rights, or to ask us to delete your account, write to info@permit.io. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, to ask us to delete it, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## Children

Phean is a tool for work and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.
