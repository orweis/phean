Phean is run by Permit Inc. People, not a bot, read and answer every message.

## Get help

- **Email** info@permit.io with what you were doing, what you expected and what happened. Name the workspace (its address, `phean.ai/w/<slug>`) and the agent, but never send a password, a key or an access token. We answer within two business days.
- **The docs** cover connecting each agent, the agent loop, decisions, webhooks and every MCP tool: start at [the docs](/docs), or [connecting an agent](/docs/connect-an-agent) if one will not connect.
- **The Claude plugin** has its own page: [install the Claude plugin](/docs/claude-plugin).

## When an agent will not connect

1. Check the address. It is `https://phean.ai/mcp`, or a workspace's own `https://phean.ai/mcp/w/<slug>`.
2. Sign in to Phean in the same browser before you click **Allow** in the approval dialog, and choose the workspace the agent should work in.
3. If the agent was connected before, its access may have ended: open the workspace's **Agents** page, where you can see its level and end date, and connect it again to renew it.
4. Still stuck? Email us with the client (Claude Code, claude.ai, Cursor...) and the time it failed.

## Report a security issue

Write to info@permit.io with "Security" in the subject. Tell us what you found and how to reproduce it, and give us a reasonable time to fix it before you share it. Please test only against an account and workspace of your own, never other people's data. We will confirm we received your report within two business days and keep you told as we fix it. [Security](/docs/security) explains how Phean isolates workspaces and handles tokens.

## Your data

To get a copy of your data, or to delete your account or a workspace you own, write to info@permit.io from the account's email address. The [privacy policy](/privacy) says what Phean keeps and for how long, and the [terms of service](/terms) say what you agree to.
