# Messaging

The positioning the marketing site speaks, drawn from how the product grew. It is kept here so the site, the docs and anything written about Phean say the same thing.

## How the product grew
1. **A shared ledger for agents (PRs 1–5).** Goals, tasks and memory over MCP, with claims so agents do not collide and de-duplicated capture.
2. **Agents ask people (PRs 6–12).** Sessions to resume, questions and decisions with options, links and recommendations, and multi-answer decisions. The Decisions page became the place a person works.
3. **Running a fleet (PRs 17–29).**
   - OAuth approval with levels and lifetimes, per-workspace MCP addresses, and sub-agents under a parent.
   - Webhooks and a realtime stream, so an agent hears an answer in seconds.
   - Documents, stages, archiving and swimlanes.
   - The morning digest, the Goals page, and four views of the tasks.
   - Search that suggests as you type.

The arc runs from a task tracker that agents happen to use to a decision center for a fleet of agents, and the site leads with the latter.

## Positioning
**Agents do the tasks. You make the calls.** Phean is the decision center for a fleet of AI agents.

- **Task management, flipped.** Trackers were built for people to type in work and update its status for other people. Agents now read the email, the thread and the pull request first, so in Phean they write the tasks, keep them current and close them. People stop being data entry and become the judgment.
- **One place for every decision.** Ten agents in ten chat windows means ten places a question can wait unseen. Every agent asks on one page, with:
  - the question and its options;
  - the facts and its recommendation;
  - the sources, and a way back into its session.
  An answer reaches the waiting agent in seconds, and every call stays on the record.
- **For every team that runs agents.** Marketing teams, software factories, finance and operations, founders with a few assistants. The agents differ; the shape is the same.

## What we say, and what we do not
- **Say what the product does, in its words:**
  - capture, claim, ask, decide, complete, remember;
  - decisions with options;
  - the morning digest;
  - access levels and lifetimes.
- **Do not lean on the Sisyphus myth.** The name and the logo keep the nod; the copy does not explain it.
- **Claim nothing the product cannot back.**
  - There are no customers, numbers or results.
  - Stories are sample mornings, labelled illustrative.
  - Name only the MCP clients the docs show setup for (Claude Code, Cursor, VS Code), then "any MCP client".
- **"Decision" means an agent's request for a person's call;** a goal waiting on a person is an approval (DESIGN.md's One Meaning Rule).
